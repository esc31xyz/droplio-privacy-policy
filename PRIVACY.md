# Privacy Policy
**Droplio for iOS**

## Your Data Stays Yours
**Droplio collects zero personal data.**

- No accounts or sign-up required
- 100% stored locally on your iPhone
- No tracking, ads, or third-party analytics
- Complete data export control anytime

---

### 1. Built for Privacy First
Droplio was created with a simple principle: your work data belongs entirely to you. We believe you shouldn't have to sacrifice your privacy to stay organized on your delivery routes.

### 2. Data Collection & Accounts
Droplio does not require account creation, registration, or sign-in. We do not collect, transmit, or store personal identifiers such as your name, email address, or phone number on any external server.

### 3. On-Device Local Storage
All data generated within the app—including workday logs, package counts, delivery routes, access codes, and gas expenses—is stored locally on your device using Apple's native storage frameworks. We do not operate external databases, cloud servers, or remote storage services.

### 4. Photo Access & Permissions
If you choose to attach receipt images or verification photos, Droplio utilizes Apple's native system photo picker. The app accesses only the specific photos you select. Selected photos are stored exclusively on your device and are never uploaded or shared.

### 5. Analytics & Third-Party Tracking
Droplio does not contain third-party analytics, tracking pixels, advertising frameworks, or crash reporting tools. Your in-app activity is never tracked, profiled, or shared with third parties or advertisers.

### 6. Data Export & Ownership
You maintain complete ownership of your data. You can export your workday and gas logs to CSV files at any time via the iOS share sheet, or remove your data directly within the app.

### 7. Security & Protection
Because your data remains on your iPhone, it is automatically protected by iOS native hardware encryption and your device security settings (Face ID, Touch ID, or passcode).

### 8. Policy Updates
We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect app enhancements or legal requirements. Any revisions will be published within this section of the application.

### 9. Privacy Inquiries
If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or data privacy in Droplio, you may reach out to us directly via our App Store product page.
